title: Distinctive solve cannot be unevidenced class leadership
---
The distinctive solve is what the product does that addresses the unmet
need. "Better than anyone," "best-in-class," and class-rank rhetoric
are illegal unless comparative_evidenced is an explicit yes backed by
primary retrieval. Competitive landscape is live retrieval only. It is
never baked into a prompt or a fine-tune.
