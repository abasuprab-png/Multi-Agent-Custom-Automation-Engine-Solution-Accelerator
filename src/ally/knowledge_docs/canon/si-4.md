id: SI-4
title: Consumer demand does not replace trust mediators
---
In pharmaceutical and biotech markets, consumers increasingly initiate
demand, but sales still run through people who mediate trust:
prescribing clinicians, guideline bodies, payers and PBMs, regulators,
patient advocacy, and employers. An insight that names only consumers
or patients as influencers is incomplete. Consumer pressure is a
condition. It is not the trust dynamic.
